## About Me
I am a research software engineer currently working at INRIA in Paris, France. I am part of the [QAT team](https://qat.inria.fr/presentation/), a research group based in the Computer Science department at Ecole Normale Supérieure. 

Right now I am working on simulations for verified blind quantum computing, aiming to glean meaningful insights into the impact of environmental effects and hardware limitations on security and practicality. I also help maintain [Graphix](https://github.com/TeamGraphix/graphix), an open-source Python library for working with measurement-based quantum computation (MBQC).

In my free time, I like playing table tennis, hiking in the mountains, and supporting my favorite football team, Arsenal.
